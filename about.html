A shop, not a scrape.

Family Deals Canada is a curated Canadian list for parents of kids about 5–10, edited from Vaughan / Toronto. The product is the short list and the Saturday email — commerce is the point, but only picks a family would use this week.

Where deals come from

There is already a local RedFlagDeals Hot Deals watcher. It does not publish a public page by itself. It writes JSON alerts from a checker script. The public, reusable feed is RFD’s own Atom: https://forums.redflagdeals.com/feed/forum/9. This site turns a filtered slice of that feed into editorial cards.

What we skip

  • Phone-plan YMMV threads and $6,000 appliances.
  • Unverified coupon stacks and expired “until 2pm today” posts.
  • Amazon dumps of random ASINs. Category shops stay labeled affiliate-soon until a live Associates tag exists.
